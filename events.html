Science Shows

Space Adventure Show

Step aboard our Space Adventure Show and journey far beyond Earth as planets spin, stars burst, and galaxies swirl around you. With vivid lights, booming sound, and larger-than-life visuals, the show brings the wonders of the universe right to your seat.

Kids become active explorers as they help solve cosmic puzzles, steer imaginary spacecraft, and discover how astronauts travel through space. It’s an exciting mix of science, storytelling, and adventure that turns every visitor into a true space hero.

How the Mind Works

Step into our How the Mind Works show and explore the incredible world inside your head. Through colorful visuals, fun demonstrations, and surprising illusions, you’ll discover how your brain sees, remembers, and makes sense of the world around you.

Kids become junior brain-scientists as they test memory tricks, solve perception puzzles, and learn why the mind sometimes plays games on us. The show mixes storytelling, psychology, and hands-on activities to reveal just how powerful and wonderfully weird the human brain can be.
