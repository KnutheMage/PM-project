Learn

Step into the Ice Age and uncover the secrets hidden inside real mammoth bones. In this Learning Zone, you’ll discover how scientists use fossils to learn about these giant, furry creatures — from how they survived freezing temperatures to what they ate thousands of years ago.

Travel millions of years into the past and see how humans evolved from curious primates into the clever explorers we are today. Follow early ancestors, observe how their bones changed over time, and discover what makes Homo sapiens unique.

Blast off into the cosmos and explore swirling galaxies, glowing stars, and mysterious planets beyond Earth. Learn how astronauts travel into space, how rockets break free from gravity, and what scientists search for when looking for life on other worlds.

Dive into a hidden world too tiny to see with your eyes. In this Learning Zone, you’ll explore microscopic creatures, cells, and particles that shape everything around us. Discover how bacteria help us, how cells build our bodies, and how scientists use powerful microscopes to reveal secrets normally invisible.
