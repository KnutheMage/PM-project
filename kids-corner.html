Science Fun Facts

Space Fun Fact
A day on Venus is longer than a year on Venus — it spins so slowly that it takes more time to rotate once than to orbit the Sun.

Human Evolution Fun Fact
Humans share about 98% of their DNA with chimpanzees, but tiny differences make us walk upright, talk, and build museums.

Mammoth Fun Fact
Mammoths weren’t just big — their tusks could grow over 4 meters long, curling like giant ice-age spirals.

Microscopic Fun Fact
A single teaspoon of soil can contain more living organisms than there are people on Earth.

DIY Experiment

What You Need

  • A shallow plate
  • Milk (whole milk works best)
  • Food coloring (red, blue, green, yellow)
  • Dish soap
  • Cotton swab

Instructions

1. Pour the Milk
Pour milk into the plate so it covers the bottom.

2. Add the Colors
Put a few drops of food coloring around the milk.

3. Dip the Soap
Dip a cotton swab into dish soap.

4. Touch the Milk
Gently touch the surface of the milk.

5. Watch the Magic
Watch the colors swirl and dance across the milk.

What's Happening?

Dish soap breaks the surface tension of the milk and reacts with the milk fat, causing the colors to move and create beautiful patterns.
